Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When are the 2026-2027 Public Exam Time Tables released?
The Directorate of Government Examinations (DGE) Tamil Nadu typically releases the 10th, 11th, and 12th standard public examination time tables in November/December. You can find the verified PDF schedule in our Exams Archive.
Are all study materials available in Tamil & English Mediums?
Yes! All study notes, SURA guides, chapter-wise questions, and textbook solutions are provided in both Tamil Medium (TM) and English Medium (EM).
Can teachers download Lesson Plans and Notes of Lesson?
Absolutely. Our Teachers Corner contains weekly lesson plans, notes of lesson, syllabus division, and administrative forms for 1st to 12th standards.
Where can I find district-wise Quarterly & Half-Yearly papers?
Navigate to the Exams Archive page where you can filter question papers and official keys by year (2019–2027) and exam category.
How do I bookmark resources for offline reading?
Click on any resource to open the PDF download modal, then click the Bookmark button. You can view, open, and manage all your saved bookmarks anytime by clicking the பயனர் (User) icon in the header.
Are NEET and competitive exam study materials updated?
Yes, our Competitive Exams section features up-to-date question banks, mock tests, and syllabus notes for NEET, TNTET, PGTRB, and NMMS exams.
